TE4
"Under the safelight, watching prints develop"
Warm, analog, intimate. The background is near-black with amber undertones. The upload zone becomes a developing tray with a thin safelight strip above it. Serif italic typography (Instrument Serif) makes everything feel hand-lettered. Buttons are pill-shaped with ghost outlines, like vintage darkroom timers. The grid breathes more: 4 columns with actual spacing and subtle borders, like prints drying on a line. 3 columns in the contact sheet because each frame is a precious print.

TE5
"Chris Marker's editing table, if it ran macOS"
Gallery installation. The upload screen centers on an eye—Marker's obsession with seeing and being seen. You drop your film into the eye. The title uses extreme letter-spacing (Playfair Display), floating in space like gallery wall text. The contact sheet switches to 3 wide columns with timecodes underneath each frame, like museum placards. The "export" button is barely there—a whispered invitation, not a command. A subtle warm vignette glows from the top. Every pixel is deliberate, nothing is decoration.
